02 / Principles

How I prefer to work.

These are not slogans. They are the habits that have helped me finish projects and communicate their limits clearly.

I would rather show the reasoning, trade-offs, and gaps than hide them behind a long tools list.

  • SHIP THEN REFINEA working system creates better questions than a perfect plan that never gets used.
  • THINK OUTSIDE-INAfter a feature works, I test it from the perspective of someone who does not share its assumptions.
  • DOCUMENT EARLYNotes, decisions, and handoff context belong beside the work, not in a rushed cleanup at the end.
  • NAME THE GAPGo, large-scale cloud security, mobile testing, and formal bug bounty practice are learning areas, not inflated claims.
