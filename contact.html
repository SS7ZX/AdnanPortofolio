FAQ

Questions I get asked directly, answered the same way.

Are you a developer who does security, or a security researcher who codes?

Both, by design. Building full-stack applications means I see firsthand where developers cut corners, which sharpens the security work. Doing security work means I write code that is harder to break in the first place.

What kind of role are you looking for?

Full-stack development, junior red team or cybersecurity, project coordination, or a hybrid role that values several of these. Open to internship or entry-level positions, remote, hybrid, or onsite in Jakarta.

What is your current experience level, honestly?

I am a 5th-semester undergraduate. My projects are self-directed and internship-based rather than large-scale production systems, and I frame them that way intentionally. What I can offer is strong fundamentals, a security-first habit formed early, and a track record of finishing what I start.

What are you not good at yet?

Automated testing infrastructure, large-scale cloud security, mobile app security, and formal bug bounty methodology. Areas I am actively working on rather than areas of strength today.

Do you take freelance security review work?

Yes. Reach out by email or LinkedIn with project scope and timeline, and I will say honestly whether it is within what I can currently deliver well.
