FAQ

Questions I get asked directly, answered the same way.

Are you a developer who does security, or a security researcher who codes?

Both, by design. Building full-stack systems taught me where engineering shortcuts appear, while security testing taught me how those shortcuts become real vulnerabilities. The two reinforce each other in practice.

What kind of role are you looking for?

I am open to full-stack development roles, junior cybersecurity or red-team-adjacent positions, project coordination work, or hybrid responsibilities where software delivery and security thinking both matter.

What is your current experience level, honestly?

I am a fifth-semester undergraduate with practical project work and internship experience rather than a long enterprise résumé. My value is strong fundamentals, direct ownership, and a disciplined approach to building and validating systems.

What are you not strong at yet?

Large-scale cloud security, mobile application security, advanced automated testing infrastructure, and formalized bug bounty pipelines are areas I am still actively developing rather than claiming as fully mature strengths.

Do you take freelance security review work?

Yes, depending on scope and timeline. I am happy to discuss a project if the work is within my current ability and I can be clear about the expected depth and constraints.
